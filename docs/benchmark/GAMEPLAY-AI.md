# Participant addendum — Gameplay AI

Read [COMMON-PROMPT.md](COMMON-PROMPT.md) and the shared brief first. All common requirements and scoring weights apply.

## Implementation focus

Deliver a complete playable project. Do not stop at asset extraction, code analysis, a design document or a suggested architecture.

Use the supplied baseline to understand the existing presentation and identify reusable ideas, but make the new industrial-fantasy identity your own. If you extract or reuse any assets from the baseline, record exactly what was reused. Do not claim ownership of third-party assets.

Prioritize a demonstrable playable slice:
- a navigable district with a clear visual landmark;
- responsive movement and actions;
- at least two recognizable enemy behaviours;
- rewards and one meaningful upgrade;
- a functioning mechanism that changes the environment;
- a boss/major encounter;
- save/continue and mobile input.

If the platform has a code-generation or preview limitation, state it immediately and provide the strongest complete source project it can actually produce. Do not substitute a concept presentation or non-interactive mockup for the playable deliverable.

## Delivery

Provide the complete source project, run instructions, asset inventory and provenance, architecture/design notes, test report, actual gameplay capture and known limitations.
